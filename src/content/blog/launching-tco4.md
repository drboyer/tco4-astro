According to my [previous "welcome to the new thecloudonline.net" post](/blog/welcome-to-the-new-thecloudonline/), the now-previous generation of my personal website launched about a decade ago. I can't say much has happened on the site since then, as I've focused much of my energy into frankly more important and interesting things, both at work and in life.

But I kept this site running all this time regardless - it's been my "home" on the internet since either 2008 or 2010 depending on how you count, and it feels good to have a personal presence on the internet. Especially these days when so much content is produced and consumed by "walled gardens" like social platforms or AI chatbots.

That said, there was a perennial item on my To-Do list to move the site away from the DigitalOcean VPS it had been running on since 2016. Not because anything was wrong with the service, but because for a site updated so infrequently, it seemed rather unnecessary to pay for a server 24✖7✖365. I would try some redesign ideas in fits and starts, checking out several different technologies over the years, but never putting all the pieces together. The third generation of thecloudonlinet.net ("TCO" for short) and that server languished on the internet.

In the meantime, agentic coding tools have roared into the spotlight in 2026 so I figured I would give them a chance to help build a new TCO for me. I primarily use Claude Code at work and find it as the generally-superior tool for agentic coding, but I know that ChatGPT tends to produce better, or at least more unique, UI designs in fewer prompting cycles. So I had ChatGPT produce the mockup of the new version of the site, iterated on it until I was about 90% of the way happy with the design, then asked it to build an actual working website based on [Astro](https://astro.build/), the static site generation framework that I chose for the next generation of thecloudonline.net. This included importing all of my old blog posts from the previous version of the blog, where I had used the blogging tool [Ghost](https://ghost.org/).

![Early mockup of TCO4 site design produced by ChatGPT](/blog/content/images/2026/11/tco4-early-mockup.png)
_An early mockup of the new site design generated by ChatGPT with some scarily on-the-nose sample posts based on my ChatGPT history 🙈._

Once I downloaded and launched the site locally, I was able to fine-tune its appearance and features using OpenAI Codex. Codex also walked me through the process of deploying the site to Github Pages, where I should be able to host it for free, without worrying about patching servers when the next [Critical Vulnerability](https://www.nsa.gov/press-room/press-releases-statements/press-release-view/article/4630238/nsa-advisory-critical-patches-recommended-for-php-vulnerabilities/) inevitably hits.

## Moving Forward

Hopefully now that blogging is as simple as adding a new Markdown file and pushing a commit to Github, that will inspire me to update the content on this site more often. Putting thoughts into writing in a refined fashion (more refined than a Tweet/Skeet/etc. anyways) is a skill worth honing in this day and age, especially as we seem to be asked to read more and more verbose output from AI models. No promises about my blogging frequency. I hear the [new Pittsburgh airport terminal](/blog/brief-thoughts-on-pittsburgh-international-airport-terminal-modernization/) turned out pretty well though 😅.
